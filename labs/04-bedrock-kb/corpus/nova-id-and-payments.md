# Nova City Services: Nova ID, Payments and Refunds

Circular GP-2026-01. Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Nova Digital Office.

## 1. Nova ID

Nova ID is the single digital identity used to sign in to every Nova City service. It is created when a resident's first permit is issued. The physical Nova ID card carries the same number and is required at Customer Happiness Centres. A lost card can be replaced online for 100 NCD and is delivered within 3 working days.

Nova ID supports two sign-in levels. Level 1 allows browsing and booking. Level 2 is required for any transaction that changes legal status, such as renewing a permit or transferring a vehicle. Level 2 requires face verification in the Nova Services app.

## 2. Paying for services

Service fees can be paid by debit or credit card, by bank transfer through the Nova Pay gateway, or in cash at Customer Happiness Centres. Card payments are confirmed instantly. Bank transfers can take up to 2 working days to be confirmed, and an application is not processed until payment is confirmed.

Instalment plans are available for fines above 2,000 NCD. The plan allows payment in up to 6 monthly instalments with no interest.

## 3. Refunds

If an application is rejected, the service fee is refunded automatically to the original payment method within 14 working days. The Nova ID card fee is not refundable once the card is printed. Refunds for duplicate payments must be requested within 60 days of the payment date.

## 4. Service channels and hours

Customer Happiness Centres are open from 7:30 to 20:00, Monday to Saturday. The Nova Services app and portal are available 24 hours a day. The contact centre can be reached on 800-NOVA, and it supports Arabic, English, Hindi and Tagalog.

## 5. Accessibility

People of determination receive priority service at all centres and are exempt from fees for Nova ID replacement. The app supports screen readers and sign-language video calls during centre opening hours.
