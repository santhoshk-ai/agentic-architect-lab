# Nova City Services: Vehicle Registration and Traffic Fines

Circular VF-2026-03. Effective 1 October 2026. Owner: Transport and Roads Authority.

## 1. Registering a vehicle

Every vehicle driven on Nova City roads must be registered with the Transport and Roads Authority. Registration is valid for 1 year. To register a vehicle the owner needs a valid residence permit or trade licence, valid vehicle insurance, and a passed technical inspection for vehicles older than 3 years.

The annual registration fee is 450 NCD for a light vehicle and 900 NCD for a heavy vehicle. Electric vehicles are exempt from the registration fee until 31 December 2027.

## 2. Selling or transferring a vehicle

When a vehicle is sold, the seller and the buyer complete the transfer together in the Nova Services app. The seller must clear all traffic fines linked to the vehicle before the transfer. The buyer must have insurance in their own name. The transfer fee is 350 NCD and is paid by the buyer unless both parties agree otherwise. After the transfer, the seller's plate can be kept for a future vehicle for 1 year.

## 3. Paying traffic fines

Traffic fines can be paid in the Nova Services app, at Customer Happiness Centres, or at registered kiosks. A 25% discount applies when a fine is paid within 30 days of the violation date. The discount does not apply to fines for serious violations, which are listed in Annex B of the traffic code.

Fines that remain unpaid for 12 months prevent registration renewal. They also block the transfer of any vehicle owned by the same person.

## 4. Parking fines

A parking fine is 150 NCD in standard zones and 300 NCD in restricted zones such as loading bays and spaces for people of determination. Parking fines can be disputed in the app within 30 days by uploading a photograph or a payment receipt. Disputes are reviewed within 7 working days.

## 5. Black points

Serious violations also add black points to the driver's licence. A driver who reaches 24 black points within 12 months has the licence suspended for 6 months. Black points expire 12 months after the violation date.
