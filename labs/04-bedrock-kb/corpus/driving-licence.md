# Nova City Services: Driving Licences

Circular DL-2026-06. Effective 15 August 2026. Owner: Transport and Roads Authority.

## 1. Licence categories

Nova City issues licences in four categories: light vehicle, motorcycle, heavy vehicle and bus. The minimum age is 18 for a light vehicle or motorcycle licence and 21 for heavy vehicles and buses. A first licence is issued as a probationary licence for 2 years. During probation, a driver who collects 12 or more black points has the licence suspended for 3 months.

## 2. Converting a foreign licence

Residents holding a licence from one of the 42 countries on the recognised list can convert it without a driving test. The conversion is done online and costs 400 NCD. Residents from other countries must complete an eye test, 20 hours of training at an approved driving school, a theory test and a road test.

The eye test is valid for 3 months and can be done at any optician registered with the Authority.

## 3. Renewing a licence

Driving licences are valid for 5 years for residents and 10 years for citizens. You can renew a driving licence up to 90 days before it expires. Renewal requires a new eye test and a valid residence permit. The renewal fee is 300 NCD. Renewals are completed in 1 working day and the new card is delivered within 3 working days.

A licence that has been expired for more than 10 years cannot be renewed. The holder must apply for a new licence and pass all tests again.

## 4. Lost or damaged licences

A replacement card for a lost or damaged licence costs 200 NCD and is issued on the same day at a Customer Happiness Centre. A digital licence in the Nova Services app has the same legal value as the card and is free.

## 5. Booking tests and appointments

Theory and road tests are booked through the Nova Services app. Test slots open 14 days in advance. A missed road test without 24 hours' notice counts as a failed attempt. After three failed road tests, the applicant must complete 10 more hours of training before booking again.
