# Nova City Services: Family Sponsorship

Circular FS-2026-02. Effective 1 July 2026. Owner: Department of Residency and Identity.

## 1. Who you can sponsor

A resident with a valid residence permit can sponsor a spouse, children, and parents to live in Nova City. Sons can be sponsored until the age of 25. Daughters can be sponsored while they are unmarried, with no age limit. Children with a disability can be sponsored at any age when a medical report confirms the disability.

Siblings, cousins and in-laws cannot be sponsored for residence. They can visit on a family visit pass for up to 90 days.

## 2. Sponsoring a spouse or children

To sponsor a spouse or children, the sponsor must show a monthly salary of at least 4,000 NCD, or 3,000 NCD plus accommodation provided by the employer. A tenancy contract registered with the Housing Registry is required, and the home must be suitable for the family size.

Documents: the marriage certificate attested by the Department of Civil Records, the birth certificates of the children, passport copies, and health insurance for each family member. A spouse sponsorship is valid for the same period as the sponsor's own permit.

## 3. Sponsoring parents

Parents can be sponsored when the sponsor can show that both parents depend on them and that no other family member in the home country can support them. Both parents must be sponsored together unless one parent has died or the parents are divorced.

The minimum monthly salary required is 18,000 NCD, or 15,000 NCD plus accommodation with at least two bedrooms. The sponsor must also pay a refundable security deposit of 5,000 NCD for each parent. The deposit is returned when the parent's permit is cancelled and they leave Nova City.

Parent permits are issued for one year at a time and must be renewed every year. Each parent needs health insurance with a minimum annual cover of 150,000 NCD.

## 4. Processing and fees

The fee for each sponsored family member is 300 NCD for a one-year permit and 550 NCD for a two-year permit, plus the 100 NCD Nova ID card fee. Family applications are processed in 5 working days. Applications can be submitted together in one request so that all family members are processed at the same time.

## 5. When the sponsor's situation changes

If the sponsor's own permit is cancelled, all family permits sponsored by them are cancelled 30 days later unless the family members transfer to a new sponsor. If the sponsor's salary falls below the threshold, existing permits remain valid until expiry, but renewal is refused until the threshold is met again.
