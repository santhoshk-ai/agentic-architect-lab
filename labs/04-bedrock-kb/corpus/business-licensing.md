# Nova City Services: Business Licences

Circular BL-2026-05. Effective 1 June 2026. Owner: Department of Economy and Trade.

## 1. Licence types

Nova City issues three types of business licence: commercial (trading goods), professional (services by qualified individuals), and industrial (manufacturing). A home-based licence is available for small professional activities such as design, tutoring and software development, provided the business has no walk-in customers.

## 2. Applying for a licence

Applications are made online in four steps: choose the activity, reserve a trade name, obtain initial approval, and submit the lease or home-business declaration. Initial approval is valid for 6 months. A trade name reservation costs 200 NCD and is valid for 90 days.

The fee for a new commercial licence is 2,500 NCD per year. Professional licences cost 1,500 NCD per year and home-based licences 750 NCD per year. Licences are issued within 2 working days after the final documents are accepted.

## 3. Renewal

Business licences are renewed every year. Renewal opens 60 days before expiry. A licence renewed late is charged a penalty of 250 NCD for each month of delay. A licence that has expired for more than 12 months is cancelled and the trade name is released.

## 4. Inspections

New commercial and industrial premises receive an inspection within 30 days of licence issue. Home-based businesses are not inspected but may be asked to confirm that they have no walk-in customers.

## 5. Closing a business

To close a business, the owner submits a closure request, clears all fines and cancels the residence permits of all sponsored employees. A clearance certificate is issued within 5 working days of the last cancellation.
