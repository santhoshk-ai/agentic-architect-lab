# Nova City Services: Residence Permits

Circular RP-2026-04. Effective 1 September 2026. Owner: Department of Residency and Identity.

## 1. Who needs a residence permit

Every person who lives in Nova City for more than 90 days in any 12-month period must hold a valid residence permit. Visitors who stay for a shorter period use a visit pass instead, which is covered in a separate circular. A residence permit is always linked to a sponsor. The sponsor can be an employer registered in Nova City, a family member who already holds a permit, or the resident themselves when they own property worth more than the property threshold set each year by the Department.

Children born in Nova City to parents who hold residence permits must be registered within 120 days of birth. Late registration is accepted but carries a fine.

## 2. Applying for a first permit

A first application is submitted online through the Nova Services portal after the applicant has entered Nova City on an entry permit. The applicant must complete a medical fitness test at an approved centre and enrol their fingerprints and photograph at a Customer Happiness Centre. The medical result is sent electronically to the Department and does not need to be uploaded.

The standard processing time for a first permit is 5 working days after biometrics are enrolled. Applications that are missing a document are paused, not rejected, and the applicant receives a message listing what is missing. A paused application is cancelled automatically if the missing document is not provided within 30 days.

The fee for a first two-year permit is 650 NCD, plus a 100 NCD Nova ID card fee. Five-year permits for property owners and skilled professionals cost 1,400 NCD.

## 3. Renewing a permit

You can renew your residence permit online up to 30 days before it expires. Renewal uses the same portal. A renewal does not need a new entry permit, but it does need a new medical fitness test when the resident is 18 or older.

Documents for renewal: a passport valid for at least six months, a recent photograph on a white background, a valid Nova ID, and proof of health insurance that covers the full permit period. Renewal applications are processed in 3 working days when all documents are complete.

The renewal fee is the same as the fee for a first permit. Renewals submitted more than 30 days after expiry are treated as late.

## 4. Late renewal and grace period

After a permit expires, the holder has a grace period of 30 days to renew without penalty. From day 31 a daily fine applies. The fine is 25 NCD for each day after the grace period, up to a maximum of 3,000 NCD. Fines must be paid before the renewal is approved. A resident who believes a fine was charged in error can raise an objection within 15 days of payment; objections are decided within 10 working days.

## 5. Cancelling a permit

A permit must be cancelled by the sponsor when employment ends or when the resident leaves Nova City permanently. After cancellation the former resident has 30 days to leave Nova City or to change status, for example by moving to a new sponsor. Cancellation is free of charge.

## 6. Contact

Questions about residence permits can be sent to the Department of Residency and Identity through the portal's message centre. The target response time is 2 working days.
